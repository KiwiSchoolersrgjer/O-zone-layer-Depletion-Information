The issue:

The issue of having the ozone layer deplete is that the thinner ozone shield is the more likely it will let more harmful ultraviolet (Like UV-C or UV-B) radiation from the sun reach the Earth's surface.

The causes and how it depletes the o-zone layer:

The main cause of the o-zone layer depletion is by manufactured chemical compounds us humans create. This includes: Chlorofluorocarbons, hydrochlorofluorocarbons, halons, carbon tetrachloride and methyl chloroform. Which when released it will have chlorine and bromine atoms into the stratosphere and breakdown the o-zone layer.

There are many things that causes this issue. Such as:
  • Chloroflourocarbons: Which were used in refrigerators, air conditioners, aerosol sprays and packing foam
  • Hydrochloroflourocarbons: Which is a temporary replace for Chloroflourocarbons but still releases o-zone destroying chlorine.
  • Methyl Bromide: An agriculture pesticide used for soil fumigations
  • Halons: Specialized chemical compounds used in fire-extinguishing systems that release highly reactive bromine.
  • Other industrial solvents: Including carbon tetrachloride and methyl chloroform

How it causes the ozone layer to deplete is:
  • Emission and rise: These stable chemicals drift slowly upward into the stratosphere without breaking down in the lower atmosphere.
  • Ultraviolet breakdown: Intense UV radiation from the Sun splits the molecules, releasing free chlorine or bromine atoms.
  • Catalytic destruction: A single chlorine or bromine atom can repeatedly break apart more than 100,000 ozone (O₃) molecules before being neutralized, destroying ozone much faster than natural processes can rebuild it

How could we be able to slow this down:
  • Service Cooling Appliances: Properly maintain air conditioners, fridges and heat pumps to prevent harmful gas leaking into the atmosphere.
  • Avoid Methyl Bromide and Harsh Chemicals: Minimize or eliminate the use of chemicals such as methyl bromide treated wopd, agricultural products reliant on restricted fumigants and aerosol products containing older propellants.
  • Choose Eco-Friendly Products: Use non-toxic household cleaners like vinegar, instead of corrosive chemical solvents and buy CFC-free pump sprays.
